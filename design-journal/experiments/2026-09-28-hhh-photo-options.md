# Livelier photos on Head, heart and hands

## Hypothesis

Chapter 03 reads as text, still photo, text, still photo. A different treatment of each part's photo can add life without adding blocks or breaking the reading order.

## Approach

Seven options at `/hhh2`, each on the same part (Head) so only the photo changes: curtain (opens from a slit on scroll), drifting plane (photo and plane move at different speeds), spotlight (grey photo with a circle of colour that follows the pointer, sweeping once on arrival), photo stack (three prints; choose the top one to see the next), moving photo (the original build's sticky notes clip, playing only on screen), photo per paragraph (a pinned photo that changes with each paragraph; breaks the one-column rule, as a comparison), and assembling tiles.

## Result

All seven work in the browser with no console errors; each respects reduced motion. Jason chose the drifting plane for every part, asking for it more subtle and for each plane to differ. He liked the moving photo too and asked to keep it for the next version.

## Conclusion

A small scroll-linked drift between photo and plane is enough to lift a page of still photos, provided it is subtle and varies from photo to photo. On chapter 03 each plane now has its own corner, colour and drift (Head top-left pale blue diagonal, Heart bottom-right terracotta vertical, Hands bottom-left warm grey sideways), at about half the demo's movement. Saved for the next version: the moving photo (the post-its clip, `public/videos/postits.mp4`).
