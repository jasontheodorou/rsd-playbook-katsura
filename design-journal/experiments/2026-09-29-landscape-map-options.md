# The Design Landscape as a living map: five options at /landscape

## Hypothesis

The hand-drawn Design Landscape (landscape1.svg) can carry chapter 04's five layers better than nested frames. The reader chooses a part of the drawing itself, and the drawing gains life from its own parts: its lines, its zones and its people.

## Approach

- The raw SVG is kept path for path in `src/app/(public)/landscape/art.ts`, generated by `scripts/landscape-art.mjs`.
- The 33 people (a head and a body each) are orange #dc7038, sampled from landscape2.png and matched one to one against its figures.
- The zones are traced along the drawn contour lines, and inner zones sit on top so they take the pointer.
- Choosing a zone shows its name on a short bar at the top of its line, with the manual's sentence below. Every state sits in one grid cell.
- A first-view cue washes the zones from the centre out, once, until the reader chooses one.
- The five treatments are Wash, Draw the line, Focus, Build and Ripple.

## Result

All five work at 1440, 1024 and 390px with no overflow. The zones work by keyboard as a radio group with arrow keys. Reduced motion shows the finished state, and without JavaScript all five names and texts show. At 390px the drawing is 350px wide, so the zones are small to tap.

Failures on the way:

- The outer zones first bulged into the sky, until they were traced along the upper contour lines.
- The first ripple was a filled disc that faded before it could be seen.

## Conclusion

Jason chose 03 Focus ("I love Focus"). He asked for two changes, and both were made.

- **Wash colour:** the chosen area's wash is now a very light blue, to set off the orange people. It is the palette's blue, #619cba, at 12% over the paper (about #e9f0f2), and 6% on hover.
- **Office block:** only the part below the table's back edge took the wash, so its top half looked unfilled. The Environment zone is now the table top joined with the full outline of any shape that rises past its edge (the office block and its tree). This outline is traced from the drawing, so the wash fills the building evenly and clicking its top chooses Environment.

## Later refinements (same day)

- **Names:** the underline beneath each zone name was removed.
- **Text:** the text now sits in the old build's tint box, a softened pale blue (#cbd9da at 45%) with 24px corners, 16px under the table on columns 2 to 11.
- **Dormant lines:** in Focus the five lines are hidden, and the 14 breaks they cross are closed with bridges measured from the drawing. Choosing a zone parts its breaks and raises its line through them. Leaving it lowers the line and closes the breaks.
- **What remains visible:** each bridge meets its pieces with a faint change in width at about 4x zoom. At page size the joins read as part of the hand-drawn line.

## Outcome

Approved and placed on How we think as the `landscapeMap` block, in place of the nested-frames `landscape` block (decision 027).
